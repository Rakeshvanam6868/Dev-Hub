# 🎥 Reel #2 — Don't Build Everything Yourself

**Duration:** ~43–45 seconds  
**Core Idea:** Build the differentiator. Use the commodity.  
**CTA Keyword:** `STACK`  
**CTA Resource:** [Modern Mobile Dev Stack Checklist](../checklists/modern-mobile-dev-stack.md)

---

## Script

### 0–4s — 🔥 HOOK
> **Face camera:**  
> "If I were building an app from scratch today, there are a few things I wouldn't build myself."

**Text overlay:** `DON'T BUILD EVERYTHING YOURSELF`

---

### 4–9s — TENSION
> "Not because I can't…"  
> "but because I'd rather spend my time building what actually makes the product different."

---

### 9–17s — PEAK → EXAMPLES
> **Show Pulsezy + quick cuts:**  
> "Mobile app? React Native with Expo."  
> "Backend and auth? Supabase."  
> "Database? Postgres."

*Keep each one 1–2 seconds visually.*

---

### 17–24s — 🔥 SECOND PEAK
> **Show RevenueCat → Sentry → PostHog.**  
> "Subscriptions? RevenueCat."  
> "Errors? Sentry."  
> "Analytics? PostHog."  
> "Why rebuild any of that?"

---

### 24–31s — REVEAL
> **Face camera:**  
> "The real decision is knowing what you should build…"  
> *(Pause.)*  
> "…and what you should just use."

---

### 31–38s — PRODUCT PROOF
> **Show Pulsezy.**  
> "For Pulsezy, I'd rather spend my engineering time on the adaptive training system…"  
> "…the part users actually came here for."

---

### 38–45s — PAYOFF + CTA
> "So when you're choosing a stack, don't ask…"  
> "'What technologies should I use?'"  
> "Ask: 'what actually needs to be unique?'"  
> "Comment STACK and I'll send you the checklist I use."

**End screen:**  
`BUILD WHAT'S UNIQUE.`  
`USE WHAT'S ALREADY SOLVED.`

---

## Notes
- One idea, not seven
- Flow: Hook → tension → examples → principle → Pulsezy proof → useful takeaway → CTA
- Target audience: developers and founders
- The viewer gets an actual rule they can use tomorrow
